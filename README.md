Sum up 27 November's world news in one image — a front page drawn by agents:
**36 stories · 11 regions · 14 fields**, every one dated Fri 27 Nov 2026.

- **3 lead stories, drawn:** a Thanksgiving storm stops Macy's 100th parade
  (a first — power cut to 500,000+, flights cancelled) · Trump seeks a probe
  into 'fake' US GDP figures after growth is revised to 4.3% · Israel kills
  10 in Gaza and Hamas weighs its reply.
- **33 more stories** in the list, each with a drawn pictogram and a
  how-sure label (CONFIRMED / REPORTED / CLAIMED): China's tariff revenge,
  Zelensky's BBC interview, UK migration at its lowest since 1997, a tanker
  capsized off Ibiza, Depardieu to stand trial, Brazil snubs Milei, the
  World Chess Championship, India's Supreme Court and Cabinet, China's
  drones, Musk's SpaceX shares, Myanmar and the ICJ, a cheaper HIV jab for
  Africa, and more — plus a UTC hour-by-hour timeline, a world map with
  pins for all 11 regions, and a DATE CHECK box.
- **How sure:** every story carries a label — CONFIRMED where the outlet
  verified it, REPORTED where it is as reported, CLAIMED where it is one
  side's claim. Nothing here is from an earlier day.

The image is hand-drawn with PIL (Python, standard library only for the
entry itself). `main.py` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
